The morning sun rose slowly over the quiet harbour and the fishermen began to prepare their boats for the day. Gulls circled above the water while the first ferry left the pier, carrying workers towards the city on the other side of the bay. Nobody seemed in a hurry, because the sea had been calm for several days and the weather forecast promised more of the same.
Secret writing has a long history. Ancient generals shifted the letters of their orders to confuse the enemy, and merchants hid their prices from rivals with simple codes. Every new cipher was believed to be unbreakable until a patient mind discovered that language itself leaves traces: some letters appear far more often than others, and certain pairs of letters follow each other again and again. These regularities are the weak point of every classical system.
A good experiment must be described so carefully that a stranger can repeat it and obtain the same numbers. This is why researchers publish their programs, their data and the exact settings they used, so that others may check the results and build upon them in the years to come.
